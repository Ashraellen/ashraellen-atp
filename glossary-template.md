# Public Glossary Template

The glossary records human-authorized terminology decisions that must remain stable across units, sessions, and tools.

| Source term | Target term | Status | Scope | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| example | example | PROPOSED / LOCKED / SUPERSEDED | global / character / chapter | rationale if needed |

## Rules

- `PROPOSED` terms are not canonical.
- `LOCKED` terms are authoritative until explicitly changed.
- `SUPERSEDED` terms remain visible for provenance but are not current choices.
- Near-synonyms are not interchangeable when distinctions carry literary or conceptual function.
- Capitalization, punctuation, grammatical number, and register may be part of the decision.
- An entry may prohibit a tempting alternative that would flatten or distort the work.
