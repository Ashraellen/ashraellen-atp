# Limitations and Failure Modes

ATP reduces recurring risks; it does not eliminate literary judgment or guarantee correct output.

## Known failure modes

### Literalization
The target preserves words while losing literary function.

### Voice flattening
Distinctive rhythm, density, awkwardness, irony, or pressure becomes generic fluent prose.

### Terminology drift
Recurring concepts are replaced by plausible near-synonyms, gradually changing the work's conceptual system.

### Dialogue convergence
Different characters become stylistically similar because the target language is optimized toward one neutral register.

### Explanation creep
Metaphor, ambiguity, or compressed thought is expanded into reader-friendly explanation.

### Local optimization
A sentence improves in isolation while damaging chapter-level or corpus-level continuity.

### Context reconstruction error
A new session or tool infers missing project state instead of restoring authorized state from durable records.

### False finality
Draft, approved, final, assembled, and published states are conflated.

### Automated-confidence error
A confident model response is mistaken for evidence of fidelity.

### Human fatigue
Human authority can become nominal if the reviewer is overwhelmed and accepts output without meaningful evaluation.

### Constraint overload
An excessively large or contradictory rule set can hide priority conflicts or create mechanical prose.

### Model dependence
A workflow relying on undocumented behavior of one model may fail after model or tool changes.

## What the protocol cannot decide

No protocol can mechanically determine the definitive meaning of intentionally ambiguous literature, whether a risky stylistic departure is artistically superior, whether an author should change their own canon, whether a culturally adapted solution preserves identity, or whether a translation is aesthetically successful for all readers.

These remain human literary judgments.

## Evaluation caution

Traditional translation metrics may measure useful properties but cannot by themselves establish authorship continuity, dialogue temperature, or preservation of deliberate stylistic resistance.

## Ethical limitation

ATP should not be used to imitate a living author's style for unauthorized derivative production and then present imitation as authorship preservation. Preservation begins from an authorized source and an authorized human authority chain.

**The method is a control system for judgment, not a substitute for judgment.**
