# Contributing

Thoughtful issues, research references, implementation reports, and reproducibility feedback are welcome.

The Ashraellen Transcreation Protocol distinguishes contribution from canonical authority. A proposed change may improve the protocol without becoming authoritative automatically.

When proposing a change, please state:

- the problem being addressed;
- the protocol component affected;
- an example or failure case where possible;
- whether the proposal changes terminology, validation logic, responsibility boundaries, or reproducibility assumptions.

Do not submit copyrighted literary material that you do not have the right to publish. Do not submit private prompts, credentials, correspondence, personal data, or confidential production archives.

By contributing documentation to this repository, you agree that accepted contributions may be distributed under the repository's CC BY 4.0 documentation license.
